Careers

​Space Lab Technologies, LLC (Space Lab®) provides new technology development, research, and engineering support services for space science and exploration systems. Space Lab® is always looking for people who are excited about working in the space industry and want to work on innovative projects. We are interested in people who enjoy being at the forefront of new discovery, as well as learning and solving challenging technical problems. We accept all resumes, but specifically look for scientists and engineers ranging from bachelors to doctorate with experience ranging from 1-20+ years. Candidates with multidisciplinary interest of space systems engineering, the space environment, life support systems, and space sciences are particularly valuable to our team.  Candidates must be US Citizens or US permanent residents.  We encourage you to submit your resume for possible alignment with upcoming projects.
